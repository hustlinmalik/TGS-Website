## We don't just dump it

A common worry before a cleanout is simple: "Where does all of this actually go?" The honest answer matters, because responsible removal is part of doing the job right.

## Usable items are donated

Furniture, tools, sporting goods, and household items in working condition are sorted and donated to local centers such as **Goodwill** and **Habitat for Humanity ReStore**. This keeps usable goods out of landfills and supports the community.

## The rest is handled responsibly

Items that can't be donated are recycled where possible or disposed of legally and responsibly. We follow proper disposal rules for bulky items, appliances, and any material that needs special handling — so nothing comes back on you.

## Why it matters

Choosing a reputable, insured company that disposes of material the right way protects you and the environment. With The Garage Standard, you get a clean garage and the peace of mind that your old belongings were handled the right way.
