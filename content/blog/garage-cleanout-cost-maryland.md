## The short answer

There's no one-size-fits-all price for a garage cleanout, and that's a good thing for you. Pricing is based on the **amount of stuff and the size of your space**, so you only pay for the job you actually have — never a flat fee that overcharges a small job to cover a big one. Our rates are low, and **we'll beat any written quote** you've received.

## What drives the price

- **Volume of material** — the biggest factor. A garage packed floor-to-ceiling takes more work to clear than one that's half full.
- **Type of items** — heavy items (appliances, construction debris) and items needing special disposal take more effort.
- **Size of the space** — a single-car garage is a smaller job than a deep two-car or detached garage.
- **Access** — tight spaces or stairs can add time.
- **Add-on services** — pairing a cleanout with organization or a refresh changes the total.

## Why we don't publish flat prices

Flat "packages" sound simple, but they almost always mean someone overpays. By pricing each job on its real scope, we keep costs fair and keep our rates low. You'll always get a clear, all-in quote up front, and we never begin work without your approval.

## How to get your number

The fastest way to know your real cost is a **free, no-obligation quote**. We look at the space, talk through what stays and what goes, and give you a firm price on the spot — and if you have a written quote from someone else, bring it, because we'll beat it.
